Choosing a branching strategy shapes how your team collaborates.

## Trunk-based development
Short-lived branches merged into `main` frequently. Great with CI/CD.

## GitFlow
Long-lived `develop` and `release` branches. Useful for versioned releases.

Pick the simplest strategy that works for your team.
